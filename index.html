FAQ

The honest answers.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes. Outdraft is a new app and isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows this, as it does for many small tools. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Windows protected your PCMicrosoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting… More info
Is it safe for my account?

Outdraft never touches the game's memory or files and never sends input to the game. During the draft it looks at the hero portraits on your screen, and it learns the game stage from Dota's official Game State Integration.

Why borderless window mode?

The overlay is a normal window on top of the game. In exclusive fullscreen Windows can hide it, and the draft can't be seen. Switch Dota to borderless window in its video settings.

What does it collect?

Anonymous usage stats only, and you can turn them off in the app. Details are in Privacy below.

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta. Tell us what you think through the feedback form.

Privacy

What leaves your PC, and what doesn't.

Usage stats

App starts, recognized drafts, opened builds, errors, app and Windows version, screen size and a random install ID. Sent to PostHog on EU servers, without your IP address. Never your Steam account, heroes, names or screenshots.

Turn it off with Usage stats at the bottom of the app.

Screen

The draft is read on your PC. Screenshots are never saved or sent anywhere.

Bans tab

Only if you add your Steam account: our server loads your public profile and hero stats from STRATZ to suggest bans, and keeps a cached copy. Clear removes the account from your PC.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, the error text is sent to our server so we can fix it. Folder paths with your user name are removed first.
